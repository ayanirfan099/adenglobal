A LITTLE CLARITY

Before you
set off.

Have something else on your mind?
We’re here to talk

Can you guarantee a visa approval?

No. Visa decisions are made solely by the relevant embassy, consulate or immigration authority. Consultancy can help you understand requirements and prepare; it cannot guarantee approval.

What should I share in my first enquiry?

Share your name, destination, service, intended dates and a brief outline of your plans. For group travel, include the approximate group size; for study, your intended course and intake. Please do not send passports, bank statements or other sensitive documents in your initial enquiry.

How much do your services cost?

Fees depend on the service and your requirements. Request a consultation to discuss the scope and obtain a quotation. Confirm consultancy fees, third-party charges, inclusions, exclusions and cancellation terms before proceeding.

Which destinations can I enquire about?

Tell us where you want to go. We’ll discuss whether we can assist with your destination and current requirements before you make any commitment. Destination availability and entry rules may change.

What is included in five-star Umrah?

Arrangements are discussed individually. Ask for written confirmation of hotel names and ratings, stay dates, room type, transfers, visa-related services and all inclusions and exclusions before confirming a plan.

Does this form send or store my enquiry?

No. The form prepares a message and opens WhatsApp. You can review and edit it, then choose to send it there. This website does not submit your form to a server or store it. Your use of WhatsApp is subject to its privacy terms.
